## Runtime contract

- LRASPP classes: background, pedestrian, vehicle, obstacle
- painted point order: x, y, z, intensity, background, pedestrian, vehicle,
  obstacle, unknown
- PointPillars classes: Pedestrian, Vehicle, Obstacle
- detector range: x/y `[-30, 30]` m
- segmentation resize short side: 384 pixels
- production online preprocessing: torchvision/PIL-compatible, four CPU workers

The earlier 12-epoch checkpoint remains an archived apples-to-apples research
baseline; it is no longer the launch default.
